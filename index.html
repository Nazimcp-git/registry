Nishan Alumni Register – Darunnajath Islamic Complex, Vallappuzha, Palakkad, Kerala, India

The Nishan Alumni Register is the official alumni portal of Darunnajath Islamic Complex (DNIC), a premier Islamic educational institution located in Vallappuzha, Palakkad district, Kerala, India. Established in 2001, Darunnajath is the first Darul Huda Islamic University affiliated institution in Palakkad. The Nishan register allows former students and graduates (alumni) of Darunnajath to register their profiles, update their personal, academic, and professional information, and stay connected with the growing Hudawi alumni network. Alumni can log in using their admission number at darunnajath.in/nishan. Courses offered include Secondary, Senior Secondary, Degree, and Post-Graduation programs in Islamic and secular streams.
